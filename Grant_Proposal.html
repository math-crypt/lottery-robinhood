2. The Problem

Traditional "reward" and "reflection" tokens suffer from two major flaws:

  • The "Dumping" Effect: Taxes are usually collected in the native ERC20 token. When the contract swaps these accumulated tokens for ETH to distribute rewards, it creates artificial, massive sell-pressure on the chart, destroying investor confidence.
  • Rewarding Passive Whales: Traditional systems reward users proportionally to their holdings. This discourages trading and active participation, reducing the overall volume and fees generated for the underlying DEX and blockchain.

3. The Solution: $IRL & Uniswap V4

Internet Robin Lottery ($IRL) solves these issues by completely removing the tax logic from the ERC20 token and embedding it directly into a Uniswap V4 Hook.

By utilizing V4's native architecture, we introduce a frictionless Swap-and-Liquify mechanism and a volume-based gamified reward system.

Key Features:

  • Zero-Sell-Pressure Taxes: The V4 Hook intercepts swaps and extracts a 3% tax directly in WETH. The $IRL chart is completely protected from contract dumps.
  • Volume-Based Rewards: Instead of rewarding passive holders, $IRL rewards active traders. The Hook tracks hourly volume on-chain and maintains a dynamic Top 10 leaderboard.
  • Automated Airdrops: Using Chainlink Automation (Keepers), 50% of the hourly WETH pot is automatically airdropped to the Top 10 traders, and 50% to all other active traders in batches.
  • NFT Lottery Tickets: Every time a user reaches 0.1 ETH in trading volume, the V4 Hook automatically mints them an ERC721 NFT Lottery Ticket.
  • Provably Fair Draws: A daily Chainlink VRF request selects a winning NFT tokenId, awarding the owner the daily ETH lottery pot.

4. Value to the Ecosystem

This project serves as a flagship demonstration of what Uniswap V4 Hooks can achieve.

It goes beyond simple dynamic fees by showcasing:

  • Real-time on-chain Leaderboards embedded in a Hook.
  • Cross-contract interactions (Minting ERC721s directly from a DEX swap).
  • Seamless integration with Chainlink Oracles (VRF and Automation) to create a self-sustaining gamified ecosystem.

Furthermore, because rewards are exclusively tied to trading volume, $IRL is designed to generate massive transactional volume and network activity for the Robinhood Chain and the Uniswap Protocol.
